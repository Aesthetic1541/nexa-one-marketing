LIMITATIONS

Use the model as evidence, not as a guarantee.

The dataset does not include product price, competitor advertising, distribution, promotions, consumer demographics, seasonality or other potentially important drivers. The regression therefore identifies statistical associations. Future allocation decisions should be validated with geographic holdouts, A/B tests, campaign-level ROAS and incremental-sales measurement.
